Hi! I'm a 3rd year Ph.D. student at the Graduate School of Data Science, Seoul National University, where I work in the VIP Lab advised by Prof. Joonseok Lee. I also completed my M.S. in Data Science in the same lab.

My research focuses on generative modeling, particularly diffusion-, flow-, and control-based image and video generation. Recently, I have been working on reward-steering of diffusion models, long video generation and world models.
